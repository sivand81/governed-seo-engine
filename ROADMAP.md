# Product roadmap (planned, not implemented)

The public v0.2 preview includes local HTML audits, source-grounded proposals, strict governance checks, Product JSON-LD, human approval records and tests.

- **v0.3:** bounded, opt-in URL fetching with robots.txt compliance, SSRF defenses, crawl budgets and reproducible reports.
- **v0.4:** bulk local catalog analysis, deterministic exports, source provenance and duplicate detection.
- **v0.5:** optional AI proposals; untrusted AI outputs independently validated; no fabricated claims.
- **v1.0:** production-ready authentication, audit persistence, manual publishing adapters, dry-run diffs, rollback.
- **v1.5:** multi-site configuration, optional schedules, monitoring, approval queue and notifications.
- **v2.0:** plugin interface, multi-tenant controls, extensibility, policy versioning and observability.

All features beyond the v0.2 preview require implementation, tests, threat modeling and review. No schedule or availability is guaranteed.
